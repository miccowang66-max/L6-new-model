2. Core Modeling Results

Model Features Included Test R² Test RMSE

2 ⭐ R&D + Marketing (Selected) 0.9474 $8,198.80

**Model Selection Justification:** Adding features beyond R&D Spend and Marketing Spend (e.g. Administration or State location dummies) causes **overfitting**. Test RMSE rises from **$8,198.80** to **$9,137.99**, while R² falls. Model 2 represents the elbow point.

8. Key Findings & Strategic Insights

  • **R&D Spend Is the King**: R&D expenditure is the single most important predictor of profitability, explaining over **94%** of variance alone. Investors should prioritize R&D capacity when screening startups.
  • **Marketing Offers Marginal Value**: Marketing expenditure contributes a slight performance boost (lowering RMSE by **$76**), but only when combined with R&D Spend. It is a secondary optimization lever.
  • **Reject Administrative Noise**: High administrative overhead does not scale with profits. Likewise, location (New York/California vs. Florida) has negligible impact. Keep models simple to prevent overfitting.
